# AFTERGLOW — Collectible Lore Fragments v1.0

> Companion to §9 (World, Arenas & Level Design — "environmental storytelling"). Twenty optional pickups that piece together **how the city actually went dark**. None gate progression; all of it rewards looking around.
> **Voice law:** these are the game's quiet half. Where death messages joke, lore fragments ache. Dry wit allowed; punchlines forbidden.

---

## How they work

- **Presentation:** drifting page-motes near landmarks (benches, tram stops, furnace mouths, mirror struts) — same pickup language as XP gems but white-gold, slower, unmissable-if-close.
- **Reading:** collected fragments open instantly in a letterbox overlay; archive lives in the menu under **Archive** (re-readable forever).
- **Distribution:** Promenade ×8 · Undercity ×7 · Spire ×5. Read in any order; the *archive* orders them chronologically regardless.
- **Telemetry:** `lore_found{id}` — completion % feeds a future community goal slot (LiveOps M6+).

# THE FRAGMENTS

## PROMENADE — *the night it went out* (1–8, civilian voices)

**01 · Festival Notice** *(city kiosk, plaza entrance)* — municipal print
> By order of the Office of Light: the **Festival of First Light** begins tonight. Founded 1892 — one hundred thirty-four years of shining. Every household, every storefront: leave your lights burning till dawn. Let the darkness know we were here first.

**02 · Postcard, unsent** *(bench by the fountain)* — tourist hand
> You can read a watch face at midnight here. Maro says the whole promenade runs on one circuit, which can't be true, because when one lamp sneezes the rest don't even blink. Tomorrow: the Spire. Apparently they can see its glow from the sea now.

**03 · Complaint Letter, draft** *(apartment window ledge)* — resident hand, crossed out twice
> Third notice. My bill has doubled since the Mirror went up, and for what — so the sky can be bright at 2 a.m. in case anyone important looks up? We pay for light twice in this city: once to make it, once to admire it.

**04 · Child's Diary, torn page** *(schoolyard fence)* — pencil, careful letters
> Day 4 of the festival. Our streetlamp went OUT, mum said don't stare, but I stared, and it BREATHED back on. Nobody believes me. The lamps breathe. When everything else is asleep the lamps are still doing something. I think they're staying awake for us.

**05 · Grid Operator Bulletin** *(kiosk, transit side)* — official
> Festival load projections remain comfortably within tolerance. The grid has never been happier, and neither should you be. Note: the Mirror Array draws are normal and temporary. Repeat: temporary. Enjoy the lights. — Operations

**06 · Service Suspension Card** *(tram shelter)* — transit print
> EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY: all tram lines suspended until further notice. Until the lights return, the transit authority advises citizens to remain indoors, keep a lamp lit, and — if you must travel — walk toward light of any kind.

**07 · Lamp Maintenance Log, final entry** *(promenade's oldest lamp)* — work-worn hand
> Unit #7. Oldest filament in the district. Scheduled for retirement again; refused again. She doesn't flicker like the others — she *holds*. Recommendation attached for the fourth time: leave her on. Some things shouldn't be switched off. — R., night crew, 22 years

**08 · Evacuation Flyer** *(plaza statue base)* — hasty reprint, ink still sharp
> IF YOUR HOME GOES DARK: walk toward any light that does not waver. Count doorways as you go. Do not follow sounds that know your name. Do not walk toward voices. The lamps will hold. The lamps have always held. — Office of Light, emergency series

## UNDERCITY — *what it cost* (9–15, worker voices)

**09 · Foundry Shift Log** *(catwalk above cold furnaces)* — grease-thumbed print
> Order from above: burn the night shift hotter, the Spire wants more. Fine. But write this down — the metal sings now. High and thin, like something far away learning our names. Not a fan of the new schedule. Neither is the metal.

**10 · Engineer's Memo, unsent** *(break room table)* — graph paper
> Forty nights at 112% draw. Reserves: zero. Everyone upstairs calls it "surplus confidence." We aren't generating light anymore, colleagues. We are lending it. And I have not found a lender in this city who forgets a debt.

**11 · Tunnel Incident Report** *(sealed tunnel mouth)* — filed, stamped FATIGUE
> Crewman D. reports shadows in Gallery 9 moving against the lamplight — "walking upstream," his words. Two witnesses, both rested, both sober. Recommendation stands as written: fatigue, festival season, overwork. Re-filed under weather. — Supervisor Vane

**12 · Union Letter + Reply Stub** *(union hall door)* — two papers pinned together
> UNION DEMAND: shut down the Mirror Array until reserves recover.
> MANAGEMENT REPLY: The Array pays for itself. The Array IS the future. Your concerns have been forwarded to the department of concerns. — [no signature]

**13 · Furnace Foreman's Final Entry** *(Ember's cold furnace)* — shaking hand
> They sent a crane for Furnace 3's core tonight — "temporary reallocation to the Spire." Thirty years she's burned. When the hook took her heart, the whole gallery went dim for a breath, and I swear on these hands she fought it. Sparks everywhere. Angry ones. Keep them, whoever finds this. Sparks remember.

**14 · Hazard Advisory** *(dark zone threshold)* — laminated, faded
> NOTICE: pools of dead light have formed where the lamps failed. They are not mist. They are not safe. Do not linger where the floor has forgotten what light felt like. Cross quickly. Count your steps. Tell someone your route.

**15 · Last Transmission, Tunnel Crew 9** *(collapsed gallery)* — radio transcript
> …say again, lamps out gallery nine to twelve, but that's not the — copy — it's not that the lights went OUT down here. It's that something finally ANSWERED them. It waited its whole life for us to blink, and we blinked, and it said — [transmission ends]

## SPIRE — *the truth at the top* (16–20, the tower speaks)

**16 · Mirror Array Brochure** *(observation deck)* — glossy, arrogant
> **OWN THE SUN TWICE.** The Mirror Array stores each day's light and returns it on request — midnight beaches, 3 a.m. gardens, daylight on subscription. Premium tiers available. Why should night happen to you?

**17 · Board Minutes, excerpt** *(executive floor)* — minutes style
> Re: Chief Engineer Osip's resignation and "grid thinning" warning. The Chair notes for the record: engineers are paid to find problems. The Mirror is paid to find customers. Motion to proceed with Festival Peak draw passes, 6–1. The Chair adds: "Dark is just light with bad marketing."

**18 · Transit Control Log** *(dead signal box, sub-spire)* — dispatcher hand
> 23:41 — Array calls for festival surge. Divert tram-grid reserve. Trams will coast; they always coast. 23:52 — Volt-line reading strange, like the current is looking back. 23:58 — give it everything, the mirror must not flicker tonight of all nights. 00:00 — [entry stops mid-word]

**19 · Spire Security Memo** *(mirror antechamber)* — quiet panic, typed
> Multiple reports: visitors standing frozen before the observation glass as the outage spread. Not fleeing. WATCHING. Night staff insist some reflections arrived a half-second LATE — moving after their owners stopped. Recommend covering all mirrors. Recommend it loudly. — Duty Sergeant, final memo

**20 · The Mirror's Self-Diagnostic** *(the mirror itself)* — printed in perfect serif
> ANNUAL SELF-CHECK: surface integrity 100%. Stored reflections: 214,006. Fidelity: perfect. Note from system: I have kept every face that ever watched me fail. They are very patient, and they do not blink anymore. They have asked me to convey that they would like to meet you. — NULL PRIME is awake.

### THE LAST PAGE *(unlocked at 20/20 — archive only)*
> The city did not lose the light. It spent it — on a festival, then a mirror, then a lie about whose job the dark was. Nobody crashed the grid. Everybody left it on. And the dark, patient student of appetites we taught for one hundred thirty-four years, simply accepted the invitation. Now a few stubborn sparks are paying the balance. Lamp by lamp. Furnace by furnace. Wire by wire. — unsigned, undated

## Implementation notes

- Fragments are `Area2D` pickups sharing the gem pooling path (§12.3); no combat interference — collection radius generous (1.5× gems).
- Each fragment = one `.tres` (id, title, body, biome, position hint); localization cap ~90 words/body.
- Archive UI lists fragments with locked entries shown as silhouettes + biome hint ("Undercity, near cold fire") — hints double as level-design tourism.
- Event `lore_found{id}` + `lore_set_complete{set}` wired to GameAnalytics; completion feeds LiveOps goal slot M6+ (`docs/liveops-calendar.md`).
