# AFTERGLOW — Devlog Content Calendar v1.0

> Companion to §19 (Launch & Marketing Checklist) and §17 (Production Roadmap). Platforms: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels — same vertical cut, three uploads.
> **Premise:** the game's glow aesthetics are algorithm catnip; this calendar turns development itself into the marketing funnel, months before any store page matters.

---

## 1. Strategy rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Hook inside 1.5 seconds** — start on the explosion, not the explanation. No logos, no "hey guys."
2. **Assume muted autoplay** — every video works with sound off; captions on everything (free: CapCut auto-captions).
3. **Series beat one-offs** — recurring formats train the algorithm *and* build return viewers.
4. **Failures outperform wins** — bugs, bad balance, and ugly early builds are the most shareable devlog content. Show them proudly.
5. **Cadence is law:** **3 posts/week** (Mon/Wed/Fri, ~19:00 local). Batch-record Sunday, batch-edit midweek — sustainable for a solo dev at ~4 hrs/week total.
6. **Never fake it:** no staged "candid" reactions, no bought views. The doc's respect-the-player law extends to the audience.
7. Every CTA is soft: *"full death-message library in bio"* beats *"LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE."*

## 2. Recurring series (the content engine)

| Series | Format | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **"Juice Check"** | 10 s before/after of one effect being added (shake, hit-stop, glow) | Transformation = retention; endless supply during P1 |
| **"Your Worst Death"** | I recreate & fix a community-submitted embarrassing death | UGC loop; comments become the idea machine |
| **"Death Message Theatre"** | Read one meme death cause deadpan over gameplay | Uses the §death-messages library as infinite scripts |
| **"Recap Card Drop"** | Generate a real player's recap card live, react to the stats | Markets §15.1 by demonstrating it |
| **"One Thumb Only"** | I play the hardest segment one-handed, on a bus (actually) | Proves the control pillar; relatable setting |
| **"Balance Crimes"** | Show an absurd broken build, then nerf it on camera | Transparency = trust; comment bait ("don't nerf it!") |
| **"Zero-Dollar Dev"** | One tool/trick per episode done for $0 (fonts, SFX, shaders) | Differentiates against funded studios; saves are high |

## 4. Hooks that work for this game (steal these)

- Start mid-explosion: *"This kill took 9 months."*
- Numbers-on-screen: *"4,112 shades attended. None sent flowers."*
- Pattern break: deadpan voice reading a death message over chaotic gameplay.
- Cost contrast: *"Big studios spend millions on graphics. Here's mine: $0."*
- Stakes: *"If this run fails, I delete the boss."* *(only when true)*

**Caption formula:** `[dry observation] + [game name] + [#AFTERGLOW]` — e.g., "The whale has eaten me 47 times. I respect it. #AFTERGLOW #roguelite"

**Hashtag sets (rotate):** core `#gamedev #indiedev #solodev` · genre `#roguelite #survivorslike #bulletheaven` · platform `#androidgames #mobilegaming` · branded `#afterglow #outshinehedark` · format `#devlog #indiegame`

## 5. Metrics & the doubling rule

Watch per video: **3-sec hold %**, completion rate, follows-per-1000-views, profile-tap rate.
**Rule:** any format that doubles your median views twice in a row gets a second weekly slot immediately; any format underperforming median for 4 straight posts gets benched, not killed — revisit after 2 weeks. Review numbers every Friday during batch-edit.

## 6. Solo-dev batch workflow (4 hrs/week)

- **Sunday (90 min):** capture raw gameplay + record 6–9 voice lines in one sitting
- **Tuesday (60 min):** rough-cut Mon+Wed posts (CapCut template preset)
- **Thursday (60 min):** finish Fri post + captions + scheduling
- **Friday (30 min):** metrics review, reply to every comment (comments are next week's content brief)
- Templates pre-built in DaVinci Resolve/CapCut ($0): intro flash, death-card lower-third, end-slate with Discord QR.
