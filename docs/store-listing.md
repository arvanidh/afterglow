## 3. Full description (~2,900 chars ✓ under 4,000)

```
The city went dark. You didn't. ⚡

AFTERGLOW is a free arena survival roguelite. You are the last spark of light
in an abandoned neon city, and the shadows have noticed. Weapons fire
themselves — you move, dodge, and choose your build. Survive ten minutes and
you'll outshine the dark. Probably.

▸ ONE THUMB, TOTAL CONTROL
A floating joystick appears wherever your thumb lands. No buttons to memorize,
no menus between you and the swarm. Play one-handed on the bus, in line, or
while pretending to listen.

▸ STRONGER EVERY 45 SECONDS
Level-up cards land constantly: new weapons, wild evolutions, game-breaking
passives. Orbiting blades, chaining lightning, laser lanes — every run is a
different machine you built by instinct.

▸ BOSSES WITH OPINIONS
A shadow whale that ate an entire district. A three-voice choir that sings back
harder when you win. And at the top of the Spire… something wearing your own
build, coming to return it.

▸ EVERY DEATH IS CONTENT
Losing generates a shareable neon stat card — your time, your build, your very
specific cause of death ("Devoured 2 seconds before your chest opened").
Instant replays save your best explosions. Failure has never looked this good.

▸ DAILY CHALLENGE: SAME RUN AS THE WHOLE WORLD
One seeded run per day, everyone identical. Streaks, global ranks, and the
quiet satisfaction of beating strangers at geometry.

▸ FAIR BY DESIGN
• No energy timers. Play as long as you want.
• No pay-to-win. Power comes from play; cosmetics come from playing well.
• Rewarded ads are always opt-in (revive, double rewards). Interstitials only
  between runs — never mid-combat, never after a boss victory.
• One purchase removes interstitials and banners forever.

▸ BUILT FOR REAL PHONES
Fully playable offline. Runs in under a minute from install to first fight.
Automatic quality presets keep it gorgeous down to budget hardware — glow,
particles, dynamic lighting included.

Free to play. Rated Teen. One permission (internet), zero accounts, zero
servers, zero nonsense.

Download now. Outshine the dark. 🌆
```
