## 4. Cross-episode rules

1. **Numbers never drift from canon:** −25%/+25%/+20% (Ember), +15%/+25% (Volt), ~600/~1200 Shards — pulled from `character-bios.md`; if balance patches change them, re-shoot the caption, not the truth.
2. **One pun maximum per episode** (Spark's watt-free zone: the dad jokes stay in his results quips unless a comment begs).
3. Every episode ends on the character's own words — unlock quote or results quip, never narrator summarizing.
4. Hashtag sets rotate per the calendar formula; branded `#AFTERGLOW` never drops off.
5. Metrics: apply the doubling rule (§5 calendar) — whichever episode doubles median views gets its format cloned for the M3 WICK drop spotlight.
6. Localization-ready: VO lines are ≤12 words each; caption overlays avoid baked idioms where possible so the DE/PT-BR passes can swap text layers without re-timing cuts.
